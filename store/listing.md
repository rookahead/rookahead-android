## Main store listing

**App name** (30 max)

    Rookahead: Chess Game Review

**Short description** (80 max)

    Free chess game reviews. Every move graded, no limits, no sign-up.

**Full description** (4000 max)

    Rookahead reviews your chess games for free, as many as you like.

    Type your Chess.com or Lichess username and pick a game, or paste a PGN. Stockfish 19 goes through it move by move and gives every move a verdict, from Legendary to Blunder. You get each side's accuracy, the moments where the game turned, and a coach's note on every move. Step through the game and try other moves with the engine running alongside.

    There's no daily limit and you don't need an account. The engine runs on your own phone, so every review is free.

    VERDICTS THAT MEAN SOMETHING
    Legendary is for a sound sacrifice that was hard to find. Clutch is for the only move in a position. Oversight is for missing the gift your opponent just gave you. The rules are public, and when they change, the games you've reviewed are graded again.

    PUZZLES FROM YOUR OWN MISTAKES
    Every Mistake, Oversight and Blunder from your reviews comes back as a puzzle, so you get another go at the move you missed. There are rated puzzles, a sprint mode, and a daily puzzle that's the same for everyone.

    PLAY THE COMPUTER
    Eight levels, every time control, takebacks and hints. The lower levels make the kinds of mistakes people make at that rating, and every game you play is ready to review when it ends.

    SHARE A MOVE
    Share a game or a single move as a link, an image or a GIF.

    YOUR GAMES STAY WITH YOU
    Games are reviewed on your device. An account is optional: make one only if you want your games, reviews and puzzle rating on more than one device.

    Rookahead is open source (GPL-3.0) and built on Stockfish, Chessground by Lichess, and chess.js.
